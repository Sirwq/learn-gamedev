Coins! Walk through every coin to pick it up. When none are left, you pass.

Find every `"Coin"` the way you found the platforms. When you overlap one, remove it from the game with `Destroy(coin)`, add 1 to a `coins` field, and write the count to the Console with `Debug.Log`, so you can see `coins: 1`, `coins: 2`, ….

**Differs from Unity C#**: C# writes `Destroy(coin);` too, and `coins++;` works in both languages. Unity games usually show the count on screen; here it goes to the Console.
