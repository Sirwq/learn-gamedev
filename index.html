Final project: two routes, one new mechanic

Rename your project, then open Level. Build a safe route and a harder shortcut to the same finish. Change or invent at least one mechanic; explain its rule in your own words.

Ready to share: both routes work from a fresh Run, failure allows recovery, controls are clear, and the Console has no errors. Save an editable project export as your backup.
