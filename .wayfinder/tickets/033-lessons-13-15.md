## Resolution

- Lesson folders `src/lessons/13-shoot`, `14-health`, `15-moving` (lesson.ts, explanation.md with a "Differs from Unity C#" note, hints.md, Reference code), added to `course.ts`. Key plans for each are in `course.test.ts`.
- **13 Shooting**: an enemy 260 px tall with no Script. Its middle is above the top of a jump, so the Lesson 12 stomp can't work. **S** shoots (Space is already jump). Player gets an `Instantiate` call. The new `Bullet.ts` (stub = empty class) flies right, Destroys the enemy and itself on overlap, and Destroys itself once it leaves the camera's view. The enemy sits at x 450 because the camera centres on the player, so the view's right edge starts at x 580.
- **14 Health and respawn**: Spikes at x 400. `Start()` saves the start position, which a field initialiser can't read. On a touch the player loses 1 health, logs it and goes back to the start, or Destroys itself at 0. Goal 1, "Get hurt and go back to the start", spots a respawn as a jump of more than 100 px back to the start x in one frame. It keeps the last x in a closure that resets on the Run's first frame. Lose events: no Player left (out of health), standing in the spikes (only seen if you don't respawn in the same frame), off screen.
- **15 Moving platforms**: water 1000 px wide over the ground clamp, so falling in loses. A new `MovingPlatform.ts`: the stub already moves back and forth by `step`. The student makes it carry the player by adding `step` to the player's position when the player stands exactly on its top. Walking along with the platform would also pass, since there is no anti-cheat.
- Not checked in a browser. Spikes and Water have no sprite, so they draw as plain boxes in their color.
